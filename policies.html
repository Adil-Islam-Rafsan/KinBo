THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Shipping & returns.

Please review these store policies before placing an order.

Delivery

Delivery charges and estimated delivery times are shown during checkout. Delivery timing may vary by location and courier availability. The shop will confirm your order and delivery details through WhatsApp.

Payment

Cash on Delivery is available where offered. If you choose a mobile financial service payment, wait for the shop to confirm the amount and official payment instructions. Never send money to an unverified number.

Returns & exchanges

Contact KinBo as soon as possible if an item arrives damaged, incorrect, or incomplete. Share your order details and clear photos so the team can review the issue. Return or exchange eligibility must be confirmed by the shop before sending an item back.

Order confirmation

Submitting the WhatsApp message sends your order request to the shop; it does not automatically guarantee stock reservation or acceptance. Your order is confirmed when KinBo replies.
